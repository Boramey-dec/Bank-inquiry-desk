How the inquiry desk works

  1. Add your bank’s documents under Data source: product sheets go in Product documents, known issues and fixes go in Issue inquiries.
  2. On Home, pick which set to search, then ask a question. Answers come only from the documents in that set.
  3. Ask follow-up questions in the same chat. Each set keeps its own conversation.

Answers are written by Claude and use your Claude usage. Check important figures against the source document.
